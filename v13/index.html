Stations
Pull lab (start: a fan of fires near and far, high and low, and a close pair), throw range (targets, crate wall, barrels), pour yard (hay, dry grass, dummies, ice), playground (bell, lanterns, fireworks).
Take
Reach toward a fire. Fires near your aim stir and brighten; the one you would grab leans toward your hand. Close your hand to grab, then yank. Or reach into a near bowl.
The post
Beside you at the pull lab: yank A (quick tug) or B (pull back), aim along your arm or the headset ray, and debug (beam and numbers).
Travel
Stand on one of the glowing circles behind you for a second.
Controllers
A or X: next station. B or Y: back. Thumbstick press: yank A / B.
